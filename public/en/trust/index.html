How We Protect Your Data

An AI knowledge base only works if you trust the process. That is why we follow this one.

1. Scope Before Access

Before we access your data, we define together:

  • The goal: What should the AI be able to do?
  • The focus: Which area is at the center?
  • In scope: Which data sources are relevant?
  • Off-limits: What is taboo?

Only once that is clear do we ask for access.

2. Minimal Necessary Access

We follow the least-access principle, in this order:

PriorityMethodDescription
1Export provided by youYou give us exactly what we need
2Restricted folderOnly the files that are in scope
3Shared working copyA copy of your data, never the live system
4Time-limited accessAccess to one tool, for a defined period
5Full accessOnly with explicit approval

We always work on a copy. We never modify your live system.

3. Sensitivity Levels

Every data source is labeled before processing:

LevelDescriptionExample
PublicAlready public or safe to reusePress releases, blog articles
InternalBusiness information, safe within the teamInternal processes, team structures
ConfidentialPrivate client, financial, or strategy informationClient lists, pricing calculations, contract drafts
Strictly confidentialPersonal, legal, or medical dataHR files, patient data, passwords, payment information
Strictly confidential data is excluded by default, unless it is explicitly required and approved.

4. Verified Facts, No AI Hallucinations

Every important entry in your knowledge base carries a source annotation:

StatusMeaning
ExtractedTaken directly from a source
InferredReasonable assumption, requires approval
ConfirmedReviewed and approved by a human
ArchivedKept for history only, no longer active

5. Humans Decide, AI Proposes

AI and automation only make proposals. A human approves before anything becomes trusted knowledge.

The moment an unverified AI recommendation lands as a fact in the system is the moment the system reproduces exactly the problem it was supposed to solve. That is why we never allow it.

6. Your Data Stays in Switzerland

  • All AI systems run on your infrastructure or in Swiss data centers
  • No data leaves the country
  • Fully compliant with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection
  • No sharing with third parties, no US cloud providers

You can pass this document on to any prospect who asks: "How do you handle our data?"
