## Core Concept

> **One-click licensing of viral organic posts as paid ad creative for DTC brands**
> *From proven post to live ad in days, not weeks*

Performance marketers at DTC brands burn thousands per month testing ad creative. Every new concept, hook, and angle is a guess until it runs. Meanwhile the creative that actually works sits in public view: organic posts on X, TikTok, and Reddit rack up 50,000+ shares proving what converts. Teams paying for studio shoots scroll right past them.

Cliprights scans social platforms for posts crossing engagement thresholds inside a brand's category. One click on a skincare video with 80,000 likes fires a personalized outreach message to its creator. Licensing, usage rights, and payment run through the platform, so a signed deal returns with legal clearance to run the post as a paid ad. Aspire and Grin own creator relationships and manage influencer campaigns end to end; Cliprights starts from the post that performs and works backward to the deal.

The scoring model is the hardest part. Share count proves attention, but whether a post converts as a paid ad stays open until a deal closes. First results train the model on which viral posts are worth chasing. Each licensed deal makes the catalog worth more to the next buyer, creating a data flywheel.

## Why Now

- $247B social media ad spend market — soaring
- Rising creative costs make studio shoots expensive vs. licensing proven content
- Increasing social media ad spend driven by platform growth
- Technological advancements in AI content analysis enable automated scanning
- Competitive landscape shifting: existing tools (Aspire, Grin) manage relationships but don't start from proven posts
- Demand for data-driven, proven content solutions in digital advertising accelerating

---

## Execution Plan

1. Wire API connections that pull posts above a set engagement threshold across X, TikTok, and Reddit
2. Onboard five beta brands; run licensing conversations by hand first
3. Pipeline logs status tags at each stage (scanned, contacted, negotiating, licensed, live)
4. Train scoring model on first deal results — which viral posts convert as paid ads
5. Automate outreach message generation and rights workflow
6. Integrate with Aspire and Grin for rapid adoption via existing creator-management stacks
7. Expand to new platforms and AI-driven content scoring
8. Open agencies and white-label partnerships to turn single-brand tool into shared UGC ad infrastructure

---

## Expansion Path

Start with DTC brand-side marketing leads at funded startups — one licensed post that beats a studio shoot covers months of subscription. From there, agencies and white-label partners convert the single-brand tool into shared UGC ad infrastructure. Each licensed deal enriches the catalog and trains the scoring model, making the platform more valuable to the next buyer. New verticals (agencies, enterprise brands, affiliate networks) and new platforms (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) widen the supply side. Long-term, the scoring model itself becomes the moat: the platform that best predicts which viral post converts as a paid ad owns the market.
