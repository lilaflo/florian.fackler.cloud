# Hormozi Playbook: Next Steps After Identifying Beachhead
## The 6 Relevant Mozi Minutes

### 1\. “The #1 Thing” — Focus ruthlessly

_"The biggest threat to your business isn't doing nothing. It's doing the second most important thing."_

Your #1 thing right now is NOT building the product. It's creating a Grand Slam Offer for executive coaches. Everything else (architecture, tech stack, features) is a second-best problem that gives incremental returns, not order-of-magnitude returns.

### 2\. “Pulling Teeth” — Get specific on pain before you sell

Before you can create an offer, you need to know the SPECIFIC pain. Not "coaches have scattered knowledge." You need: _"I spent 40 minutes looking for a client's 360 feedback from 3 months ago across 4 different tools, and I still couldn't find it. I looked stupid in front of the client."_

That level of specificity. Go talk to 5-10 executive coaches. Pull teeth. Two questions only: "Can you tell me more about that?" and "Can you give me an example?"

### 3\. “What Business Are You REALLY In?” — Question your assumption

You think you're in the "AI workspace" business. You might actually be in the "client context preservation" business, or the "team knowledge handoff" business, or the "coaching session productivity" business. The real business is defined by what constraint is actually holding your target back. You won't know until you talk to them.

### 4\. “Close Rate Pricing Guide” — Price is a diagnostic

"80%+ close rate means you're underpriced by 3-4x." Once you have an offer, test pricing immediately. If everyone says yes, you're too cheap. The correct price is one in which you hear "no" more than "yes", but you make more money.

### 5\. “The Live Demo Pitch” — Don't promise, demonstrate

_"Tiny proof beats a big promise every time."_ When you're ready to sell, don't pitch "AI-powered workspace." Do a live demo: "Give me one of your messy client files. Let me show you what our tool does with it in 60 seconds." Fast, valuable, easy to understand, impossible to fake.

### 6\. “The 20 Year Master” — Don't rush the foundation

_"You can't have a baby in one month by getting nine women pregnant."_ Building proper systems before scaling is faster than scaling without them. Slow down to speed up.

## Hormozi's Playbook for This Stage

1.  **TALK** to 5-10 executive coaches (pull teeth — get specific pain)
2.  **CREATE** a Grand Slam Offer from that pain (dream outcome, obstacles, time, effort)
3.  **SELL** it before you build it (concierge/done-with-you first)
4.  **DEMONSTRATE** with a live demo as your pitch
5.  **PRICE** high enough that some people say no
6.  **BUILD** the product to deliver on the promise (LAST step)

**The product is the LAST step, not the first. Sell first, build second.**
